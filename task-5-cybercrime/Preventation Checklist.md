Part B - Prevention Checklist
Create a 'Stay Safe Online' checklist with at least 8 specific, actionable prevention tips relevant to college students in India. 

Stay Safe Online – Checklist

•	Do not click on unknown or suspicious links
•	Always check the sender’s email address carefully
•	Use strong and unique passwords
•	Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
•	Never share OTPs or banking details with anyone
•	Always verify UPI payment requests before approving
•	Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions
•	Keep your devices and apps updated regularly
•	Report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in
•	Contact helpline 1930 in case of financial fraud
